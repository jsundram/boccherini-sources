| file | work | pages |

**Official scan commissioned from the Library of Congress** — a paid reproduction, with no
guarantee it could be ordered again, so it is declared irreplaceable in `originals.jsonl`.
Shelfmark `M452.A2 B68 Case`, taken from the filename it was delivered under. Dedication on
the manuscript: *Composti espressamente per il Cittadino Luciano Bonaparte*.

## Processed per work, 2026-09-30

The Library's capture was one PDF of 34 openings, each page an opening of two leaves,
including the wrapper and the blank leaves. The collection owner split it per quartet,
separated each opening into its two leaves, and removed the wrapper and blanks. Those two
files are what the archive holds; the unprocessed capture was moved to
`~/.Trash/boccherini-loc-unprocessed-2026-09-30/`, whose `WHY.md` records its checksum, byte
count and page count.

`page-index.md` maps every page of both PDFs back to the opening and side it came from, and
`derivation.json` is the machine-readable form, including the leaves removed by hand.

## No longer the only copy of G 248–249

Op. 64 is Boccherini's last quartet set, dated 1804 — the year before he died.

> **Corrected 2026-09-29.** This section previously said RISM catalogued G 248–249 only at
> DK-Km and Harvard, and that this was the project's only copy. Both were wrong: F-Pn
> `Vma ms 1067 (5)`/`(6)` carries them as well, and that copy is now held at
> `G248/840022759_F-Pn` and `G249/840022760_F-Pn` — photographed at the BnF in April 2026.
> This remains the only *complete* G 248 **and** G 249 in one source: the F-Pn G 249 is the
> first movement only.

The Copenhagen title page reads *"Deux Quatuor de Luigi Boccherini pour 2 Violons, Alto &
Violoncelle, Op. 64, Inédits"*; the Harvard one *"Due Quartetti Per due Violini, Viola e
Violoncello"*.

## RISM does not catalogue this manuscript

There is no RISM source record for this copy to link to. RISM records **24 US-Wc holdings**
across the quartet corpus; every one is `M452.A2 B67 Case` or carries no shelfmark.
`M452.A2 B68 Case` appears nowhere — `B67` is the printed collection, `B68` the manuscript.

So the links above point at the **works**, not at this copy. `metadata.json` records that
distinction as `rism_catalogues_this_copy: false`.

Friends' photographs of the French manuscripts of these same two quartets are held
separately and are not part of this folder.
