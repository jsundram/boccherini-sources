# G 184, 185, 186, 188 — Op. 22, manuscript (F-Pn, `VMB MS-153`)

> *Quartetti de Boccherini* — 4 parties, 21 × 28 cm, 1775–1800

Complete in four parts, but assembled from two sources.

| part | file | pages | resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violino I | `Vmb-Ms-153.violin1.pdf` | 42 | 1024 × 1536 |
| Violino II | `Vmb-Ms-153.violin2.pdf` | 38 | 1024 × 1536 |
| Viola | `Vmb-Ms-153.viola.pdf` | 40 | 1024 × 1536 |
| Violoncello | `001.jpg` … `042.jpg` | 42 | full-resolution IIIF |

**Gallica serves only the violoncello.** Its manifest has 42 canvases running `plat sup.`
to `plat inf.`, and the record itself reads *"4 parties … Nombre total de vues : 42"* — four
parts described, one digitized. Searching Gallica by title and by shelfmark returns this one
ark and nothing else. The other three part-books came from an earlier download pass and are
not obtainable from Gallica now, which is why they are lower resolution.

The covers all carry the same stamp, `MVSIQVE / Vm.b Ms / 153(1-5)`, with the part named on
a shield label.

## RISM

Collective record: [**840013688**](https://rism.online/sources/840013688) — *Quartetti de
Boccherini*, F-Pn `VMB MS-153`. RISM does **not** link Gallica's scan.

RISM's four constituent records give the Gérard numbers, and their order confirms the
earlier pass's reading of *"Op. 22 — 4, 2, 6, 3"*:

| in the manuscript | Gérard | Op. 22 | RISM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quartetto I | **186** | no. 4 | [840022733](https://rism.online/sources/840022733) |
| Quartetto II | **184** | no. 2 | [840022734](https://rism.online/sources/840022734) |
| Quartetto III | **188** | no. 6 | [840022735](https://rism.online/sources/840022735) |
| Quartetto IV | **185** | no. 3 | [840022736](https://rism.online/sources/840022736) |

### A fifth item, and a second G 188

[840022737](https://rism.online/sources/840022737) is a further record at this shelfmark —
*"Quartetto Terzo | A Due Violini Viola, e Basso | Bochni"* — also **GerB 188**. So two
records carry G 188. Whether that is a duplicate record or a second copy of the work bound
into the volume is not established here. It does account for the `(1-5)` in the cover
shelfmark: five items, not four.
