# Sei quartetti … opera X — the London Bremner edition

Printed edition from BNE Digital. **Not linked by RISM.**

- **Gérard:** G 171–176 (Op. 9, 1770)
- **Printed opus:** **X** on the title page — Bremner's own number. *Op. 9* is the
  normalized modern number the Gérard catalogue uses; the two are not in conflict.
- **Parts:** vl 1, vl 2, vla, vlc — all four present and labelled
- **Contents:** complete and in Gérard order; **incipits verified against RISM**
- **Identified by hand from the scan, 2026-09-28**

RISM catalogues this edition as [990005866](https://rism.online/sources/990005866) (*"libro terzo di quartetti … opera X"*) and records an E-Mn copy at `M/680-683` — very likely this one.

Bremner's numbering across his Boccherini issues: *opera prima* = G 159–164, *opera VI* = G 165–170, *opera X* = G 171–176.
