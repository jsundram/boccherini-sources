## Why this is no. 5 and not no. 3

The file arrived named *Opus 48 Number 3*. That is its position in the volume, which
holds only four of the six quartettini. The manuscript dates itself **mese di ottobre 1794**, and
Op. 48 is one quartettino per alternate month of 1794:

    febraro=226, aprile=227, giugno=228, agosto=229, ottobre=230, decembre=231

Ottobre is **no. 5**. The same month-to-number sequence appears
identically in two other complete copy sets — D-B `D-11167`–`D-11172` and F-Po
`RES-507 (13)`–`(18)` — so the reading does not depend on RISM's judgement or on ours.

Op. 48 nos. 1 and 2 (G 226, February; G 227, April) are **not in this volume**.
