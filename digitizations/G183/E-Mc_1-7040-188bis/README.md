# G 183 — Op. 1 of Op. 22 (E-Mc, `1/7040-188 bis`)

`E-Mc_1-7040-188bis.pdf` — 40 pages, 5 parts (violin1, violin2, viola, cello, basso), C.

> *Quartetto Iº · per due violini, viola e violoncello obligato · fatto per servizzio di
> S.A.R.le D.n Luigi Ynfante di Spagna · opera 3ª · da Luigi Boccherini, virtuoso di camera*

A Madrid court copy from the household Boccherini served, made **in 1782** from originals
dated **1775** — the year of composition. Digitized by the Real Conservatorio in 2007 and
published by the library as a PDF.

## RISM does not know this copy

RISM records **no E-Mc holding anywhere in the quartet corpus**; its only Madrid sigla are
E-Mn and E-Mp. There is no source record to link, so `rism_related` points at RISM's records
for the same work at other libraries and `rism_catalogues_this_copy` is `false`.

```sh
uv run boc.py show digitizations/G183/E-Mc_1-7040-188bis
```

## "opera 3ª" is not Op. 3

The title page numbers this set **opera 3ª**, Boccherini's own numbering. It is the set
published as **Op. 22**. The identification rests on the 1775 date, the roman numeral on
the title page, and the library's catalogue record — not on that opus number.

## Resolution

150 ppi, about 1730 × 1200 per page, JPEG 2000 — roughly a sixth of the pixels of the Berlin
scans at 300 ppi. Readable; thin for close work on the hand.
